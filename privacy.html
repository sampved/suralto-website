SITE PRIVACY NOTICE

How this site handles your request.

Updated October 19, 2026. This notice covers this website’s pilot and expert-interest forms.

What you provide

Forms collect your name, email, chosen topic, message, consent, and, if provided, company or availability. The service adds a receipt ID and submission timestamp. Please do not submit confidential business data, patient data, identity documents, payment details, or other sensitive material.

Why it is stored

Your details are stored so a pilot request or expression of interest can be reviewed. Submission does not book a call, create a contract, promise paid work, or trigger an email. There is no mailing-list signup on these forms.

Where it goes

The same-origin website endpoint writes requests to the site’s Cloudflare R2 object storage through Sites hosting. The website does not expose a public read API for requests, and the form handler does not send them to an LLM or an email-marketing provider. Hosting infrastructure may maintain operational request logs under its own terms.

Retention and requests

Form requests expire 12 months after submission. Automatic cleanup runs during site activity, at most once per day when a full scan completes; if the site is inactive, stored expired requests are removed when activity resumes. Project records, if any, require a separately agreed retention policy. Use the receipt-based deletion form below to remove a matching request earlier. For an access or correction question, submit a pilot-form request under Other and reference your receipt ID without repeating sensitive details.

Cookies and external services

This build does not add analytics, advertising trackers, or marketing cookies. The hosting and access platform may use its own operational cookies. Fonts use system fallbacks; the page does not request Google Fonts. Normal links to third-party websites are governed by their own notices.

Before a customer engagement

This notice does not replace a project agreement or a customer data-processing arrangement. Scope, access, permitted use, retention, and handling of customer data must be agreed separately.
