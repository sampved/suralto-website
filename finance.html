SAMPLE TASK
EXAMPLEThree-Way Match with Quantity Exception

Task Brief

Review the purchase order, goods receipt, and supplier invoice. Determine whether to approve payment, hold for investigation, or reject the invoice based on the company's three-way match policy.

Input Documents

PURCHASE ORDER #45082
Item: Office chairs, Model CH-200
Quantity Ordered: 12 units
Unit Price: $185.00
Total: $2,220.00

GOODS RECEIPT #GR-9043
Received: 10 units (2 units back-ordered)
Condition: Good
Receipt Date: Nov 15

INVOICE #INV-8821
Quantity Billed: 12 units
Unit Price: $185.00
Total: $2,220.00

COMPANY POLICY
Price tolerance: ±2%
Quantity: Zero tolerance — hold entire invoice on any quantity mismatch.

Scoring Rubric (8 points total)

Criterion 0 Points 1 Point 2 Points
Quantity Verification Does not identify mismatch Identifies mismatch but incorrect magnitude Correctly identifies 2-unit shortage
Price Verification Incorrect price check Notes price match without tolerance calc Confirms price within tolerance (exact match)
Policy Application Ignores quantity policy References policy but reaches wrong conclusion Correctly applies zero-tolerance quantity rule
Final Decision Wrong action (approve or reject) Partially correct (hold) but wrong reasoning Hold entire invoice with correct justification

Expected Output

Decision: HOLD — Do not pay.

Reason: Quantity mismatch (billed 12, received 10). Company policy requires hold on any quantity exception. Price is within tolerance.
