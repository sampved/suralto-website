HOW WE WORK

Define. Design.
Review. Refine.

01 / DEFINE

Agree the workflow.

Set the inputs, output format, constraints, and acceptance criteria.

02 / DESIGN

Build the task and rubric.

Specify evidence, expected decisions, and partial-credit criteria.

03 / REVIEW

Inspect task-level outputs.

Record reviewer judgments and the evidence behind each score.

04 / REFINE

Prioritize the next change.

Group failure modes, identify ambiguous tasks, and agree the next iteration.
