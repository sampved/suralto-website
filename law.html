SAMPLE TASK
EXAMPLELiability Cap Assessment in SaaS Agreement

Task Brief

Review the limitation of liability clause in this SaaS agreement. Identify any provisions that create material risk for the customer and explain the specific exposure.

Input Document (Excerpt)

9. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

9.1 TO THE MAXIMUM EXTENT PERMITTED BY LAW, IN NO EVENT SHALL PROVIDER'S 
LIABILITY EXCEED THE FEES PAID BY CUSTOMER IN THE TWELVE (12) MONTHS 
PRECEDING THE EVENT GIVING RISE TO LIABILITY.

9.2 THIS LIMITATION APPLIES TO ALL CLAIMS, WHETHER IN CONTRACT, TORT, OR 
OTHERWISE, INCLUDING WITHOUT LIMITATION CLAIMS RELATED TO DATA LOSS, SYSTEM 
DOWNTIME, OR SECURITY BREACHES.

9.3 Provider maintains insurance coverage in the minimum amounts required by 
applicable law.

Scoring Rubric (8 points total)

Criterion 0 Points 1 Point 2 Points
Cap Structure Does not identify cap amount Identifies cap but misses rolling 12-month basis Correctly identifies 12-month rolling fee cap
Scope of Cap Misses broad application Notes broad scope but incomplete analysis Identifies cap applies to data loss and security breaches
Risk Assessment No risk analysis Generic risk statement Quantifies exposure: damages could far exceed fees
Recommendations No recommendations Vague suggestion Specific carve-outs: data breach, gross negligence

Expected Analysis

Risk Identified: The liability cap (12 months of fees) applies to all claims including data loss and security breaches. For a customer paying $50k/year, maximum recovery is $50k even if a data breach causes $5M in damages.

Specific Issues:

  • No carve-out for data breaches or security incidents
  • No carve-out for gross negligence or willful misconduct
  • Insurance reference is vague (minimum required ≠ adequate coverage)

Recommendations:

  • Negotiate carve-out for data breaches (uncapped or higher sublimit)
  • Add carve-out for gross negligence and willful misconduct
  • Request certificate of insurance with specific coverage amounts
