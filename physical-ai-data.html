SAMPLE TASK
EXAMPLEMaterial Handling — Lumber Stack Transfer

Task Description

Transfer a stack of 2x4 lumber boards (varying lengths, some with bows or knots) from a staging area to a work zone while maintaining stack integrity and avoiding ground contact. Captured with chest-mounted camera and IMU sensors during a real framing operation.

Deliverable Components

  • 10-minute egocentric video (1080p60, H.264)
  • IMU data stream (100Hz)
  • Hand pose estimates (keypoint format)
  • Object bounding boxes for lumber pieces
  • Action labels: grasp, lift, carry, place, adjust
  • Scene metadata: lighting conditions, ground surface type, stack dimensions

Quality Criteria (Expert-Defined)

  • No boards touch ground during transfer
  • Stack remains stable (no pieces fall)
  • Efficient path (no backtracking)
  • Safe lifting technique (proper stance, controlled motion)
